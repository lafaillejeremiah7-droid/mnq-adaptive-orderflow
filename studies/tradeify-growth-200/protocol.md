# MNQ, Tradeify Growth $25K, November 20, 2026
## Frozen question
Which of 200 causal intraday MNQ entry policies, with the same execution and risk budget, improves net expectancy and reaches the first Growth funded payout milestone without breaching the account? Flat cash earns zero. No discretionary user trade log is available for a manual baseline. Evaluate all 200. No interim changes based on scores. The researcher defines 40 OHLCV mechanisms and five distinct decision policies per mechanism. These are 200 related trading rules; the mechanisms and variants are not independent discoveries. The catalog and signal implementation must be hashed and committed before any scoring.

## Evidence and execution
Use only dated outright MNQ OHLCV-1m files under the MNQ directory in tdtrader76/NQ_Backtest, Git revision 7ac6bd20d3fa46b25fa6d2db947200ef9860c6ef. Verify Git blobs and metadata; no NQ scaling or invented ticks. The same archive and portions of the last period have appeared in earlier repo studies. The date split protects this campaign's frozen rules; it cannot supply a globally blind replication. Partition explicit regular U.S. cash-market sessions including holidays/short days using New York time. At 10:00 ET choose the outright with the largest total volume over all 30 observed opening minutes; lexical tiebreak, no later prices used for selection. Audit every scheduled session and exclude missing/incomplete selected sessions explicitly from price tests. Only complete blocks may enter MC, and any actually missing Mon–Thu session censors a historical account that survives to it. Friday is available for indicators and is a zero-trade day by policy.

Trade Mon–Thu, at most two nonoverlapping trades per day. Signal at completed bar close, enter at following bar open at or after 10:00 ET. Exit at most 25 minutes later, no later than 14:59 ET (12:58 ET on preannounced half-days). Each trade uses a stop in index points equal to twice the mean of the latest 14 true ranges, clipped to [12,46] and rounded up to a tick; profit target = 1.5 stops rounded up to a tick. Entry must allow at least one minute before the flatten time. Brackets use the reference opening print. Stop-market fills account for adverse opening gaps. A passive profit target requires a one-tick trade-through, receives no favorable price improvement. Mark adverse prices before favorable targets when OHLC order is ambiguous; additionally record dual-touch counts. Close trades at the time-stop opening print. All-in MNQ fees $1.82 round trip; model one tick of aggregate spread/slippage per side (three ticks per side for cost stress); each tick is $0.50. No historical quotes, depth, guaranteed fills, or commissions inferred from the data. Historical quotes and recorded fills are required to validate these assumptions.

Planned risk cap $100 including stressed fees and slippage. Whole MNQ lots only, at most 10; take the largest affordable quantity. In account replays also reduce lots to fit the remaining hard-floor and daily-loss cushion, with a one-cent margin. Stop gaps can exceed planned risk. A zero-size order is skipped. Baseline performance statistics use the same stand-alone risk cap without an account liquidation constraint. No hedging, stacking, overnight positions, news-specific rule or real order placement.

## Rule snapshot
Fresh Growth evaluation $25,000. Realized net profit target $1,500, stop taking evaluation trades when achieved. Funded starts fresh at $25,000 the next trading session. Evaluation has no consistency requirement or funded floor lock. EOD floor starts at $24,000 and trails the highest EOD balance by $1,000. A touch on open net liquidation fails immediately, with conservative mark and fill conventions; never use only closed-trade drawdown. On funded the floor locks at $25,100 on an EOD balance of at least $26,100 or upon payout request. Daily-loss pause initially $600; funded next-session limit increases to $1,000 after closing at $26,500. Gaps that cross thresholds are conservative; DLL can overshoot. Regular firm deadline 16:45 ET, holiday deadline 12:59 ET. No simulator time limit from the firm; research horizons 13, 26 and 52 weeks.

Funded eligibility at EOD: net balance >= $26,500, at least five days of strictly more than $100 net (conservative for ambiguous fee treatment), biggest pre-commission daily profit <=35% of cumulative pre-commission profit. Require strictly more than half of trades and profit from trades certainly held beyond 10 seconds. Entry-minute exits have unknown duration. Use a pessimistic signed-profit bound (uncertain losses counted as long, uncertain gains as short) AND >50% of positive profits from confirmed long holds. Use actual execution-cost price P&L for gross consistency. Apply the count/profit test only in funded stage. Freeze at eligibility; money receipt, approval, KYC/review and continued balance pending approval cannot be verified historically. $250 minimum/$1,000 maximum first gross request, 90% trader share. One personal account only. A full inactive Mon–Fri week receives separate conservative inactivity stop; also run a warning-only sensitivity, since the firm describes discretionary notice/closure. Ignore incomplete activity weeks at initial funded activation; do not create token trades.

## Tests, decisions and rankings
Development through 2021-12-31. Fixed, zero-fit policies are scored in five consecutive six-month walk-forward blocks, 2022-01-01 through 2024-06-30; historical features update only as observations arrive. No tuning at fold boundaries. Final chronological confirmation from 2024-07-01 to archive end. Report all candidates across every partition; no signal/parameter is selected using final outcomes. Compute annualized daily Sharpe (sqrt(252), zero risk-free, zero-trade cash sessions included), trade-level profit factor, net expectancy, net P&L, win rate, high-water drawdown including adverse open equity and EOD drawdown, and results by regime. Ex ante session regime uses opening 30-minute range / opening price relative to the preceding 60 sessions' median (minimum 20), and the direction of that day's opening half-hour. Classify unknown explicitly.

Test the mean weekly net P&L above zero, with 19,999 centered circular four-week block-bootstrap draws and independent random seeds for walk-forward and final. Familywise Holm-adjust all 200 one-sided p-values per confirmation period. A viable policy requires walk-forward >=100 trades, at least four of five positive folds, positive stressed P&L, Holm p<=.05; then final >=50 trades, positive stressed P&L, Holm p<=.05. All thresholds are research choices. Show bootstrap uncertainty and a no-trade benchmark. The set selected before final outcomes is the walk-forward gate set. No rescue by post-hoc reversal or cherry-picked regime.

Account research: 100,000 common-random-number, nonparametric Monte Carlo paths per strategy, including rejected strategies as exploratory estimates if feasible. Resample four contiguous, fully observed historical calendar weeks from walk-forward data per block, preserving weekdays, order, trades and flat days. Simulation starts Monday and funds the next session after evaluation success; score at 13, 26 (primary) and 52 weeks including unfinished paths in every unconditional denominator. Separate evaluation pass, hard failure, inactivity, unresolved and joint evaluation-to-eligibility; report eligibility conditional on passing too. Base 26-week ranking by joint eligibility, then fewer hard failures, then eval pass, then stable ID. Repeat cost and inactivity assumptions; compare actual overlapping chronological starts in the final segment, recording missing coverage explicitly. Rank exploratory model outputs separately from statistical qualification. Narrow binomial Monte Carlo counting error does not quantify market uncertainty. No valid statistical survivor means no defensible winner can be claimed. An out-of-time paper/quote replication would still be needed before risking an evaluation fee.
