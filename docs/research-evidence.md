# Evidence for MNQ and NQ research decisions

Reviewed November 19, 2026. The exchange specifications establish how much a price move costs. The studies and institutional disclosures below explain methodological choices. None supplies a validated intraday MNQ policy, account statement, fill record, or prop-firm rule.

## Contract units and account survival

| Outright futures | Dollars per index point | Minimum increment | Dollars per tick | Price loss over 20 points, one contract |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| MNQ | $2 | 0.25 points | $0.50 | $40 |
| NQ | $20 | 0.25 points | $5.00 | $400 |

CME Rules 36102.B–C and 35902.B–C confirm both multipliers and minimum increments. Intermonth spreads have separate tick rules. The $40 and $400 examples exclude fees and slippage. [CME chapter 361 (MNQ)](https://www.cmegroup.com/content/dam/cmegroup/rulebook/CME/IV/350/361.pdf) · [CME chapter 359 (NQ)](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/CME/IV/350/359/359.pdf)

For a whole-contract proposal, charge fixed order-level costs once and variable costs on each contract. With budget `B`, fixed order costs `f`, and positive adverse loss per contract `v`, a feasible positive size must satisfy `f + n × v ≤ B`. Integer division gives the largest candidate size under these assumptions. Also enforce the verified firm's touch/breach convention, position and margin limits, and stressed loss at the proposed size. A stop can fill past its trigger or fail to fill. The adverse scenario is conditional on the specified shock. Even a correctly implemented finite stress test leaves residual tail risk.

Kelly's objective maximizes expected log growth under a specified return law. For a binary win of `b` per dollar at risk and a loss of 1, maximize `p log(1+fb) + (1-p) log(1-f)`. Differentiation gives `f = p - (1-p)/b` for an interior optimum. Long-only sizing clips a negative result at zero. Variable exits require the actual distribution of net payoffs. Incorrect probabilities or clustered losses change the result. Applying a fraction such as one quarter does not cure those problems. Busseti, Ryu and Boyd derive a tractable bound under their repeated-bet wealth model. A firm that fails a trader at a moving equity floor and imposes daily limits has different path dynamics. This project has no defensible positive growth allocation for a live account. [Kelly, 1956](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1956.tb03809.x) · [Risk-Constrained Kelly Gambling](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/pdf/kelly.pdf)

A useful future comparison would freeze a single setup with its structural stop and replay identical decision times, paths and paid costs. Compare a fixed dollar cap, a fixed fraction of verified disposable headroom, volatility-scaled size, and a drawdown-budget cap. Report net expectancy, terminal failures, daily breaches, maximum drawdown and duration underwater. Apply the same hard limits to every method. Predeclare a risk appetite and the assumed shock sizes. A chance constraint, expected-shortfall optimizer or Kelly variant requires enough independently observed tails, a defensible capital base, and holdout validation. VaR gives a quantile and does not measure losses beyond it. Expected shortfall summarizes tail severity and still misses unobserved failures. [Rockafellar and Uryasev](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~rtr/papers/rtr187-CVaR2.pdf)

## What the evidence can support

| Research question | Preferred test | Reason to abstain or defer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can a forecast beat event geometry alone? | Predefine the label, horizon and elapsed-time embargo. Pair forecasts on identical eligible timestamps. Report coverage, abstentions, Brier and log loss, reliability bins with counts and block uncertainty. | A proper score rewards accuracy and calibration, including false confidence. Different coverage can make a specialist appear better. Sparse bins and overlapping labels defeat independent-bin intervals. [Gneiting and Raftery](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf) |
| Does a policy earn enough after costs? | Evaluate the mean signed, realized or explicitly simulated net payoff per decision and per session. Include flat sessions, no fills, time exits, losses and costs for each side. Stress spread, delay and adverse stop fills. Pair with a frozen benchmark at equal exposure. | A target-first frequency alone omits payoff sizes, censoring and friction. Simulated bar fills cannot establish manual execution performance. [Almgren and Chriss](https://doi.org/10.21314/JOR.2001.041) |
| Is a promising Sharpe estimate credible? | Use a consistent return interval and risk-free convention. Examine skew, kurtosis, autocorrelation and uncertainty. PSR compares an observed Sharpe with a fixed threshold. DSR raises that threshold for the search using cross-trial dispersion and effective independent trials. Minimum track record depends on the threshold and effect size. | A single chosen Sharpe, unknown failed trials, a near-zero denominator or serial dependence preclude a convincing DSR claim. Annualization by the square root of periods needs its own dependence assumptions. [Bailey and López de Prado, equations 1–3](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf) · [Lo](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/research/financial-analysts-journal/2002/the-statistics-of-sharpe-ratios) |
| Did tuning create the winner? | Register all variants before selection. Purge training labels whose outcome interval reaches the evaluation boundary. Freeze the selected policy, embargo and information availability, then unlock one chronological holdout. Any redesign needs a fresh holdout. | Probability of backtest overfitting needs a time-by-candidate performance matrix. White's Reality Check and Hansen's SPA require comparable benchmark loss differentials across the candidate family and an appropriate resampling law. Four scores from one tournament cannot supply these inputs. [Backtest overfitting](https://escholarship.org/content/qt4hn4t174/qt4hn4t174.pdf) · [PBO methodology](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf) · [White](https://doi.org/10.1111/1468-0262.00152) · [Hansen](https://doi.org/10.1198/073500105000000063) · [López de Prado, ch. 7](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Advances%2Bin%2BFinancial%2BMachine%2BLearning-p-9781119482086) |
| Could dependence or changing regimes explain the effect? | Resample contiguous session blocks, vary block length as a sensitivity check, and also replay the original chronological path. Inspect contributions by contract, calendar period and volatility regime defined at decision time. | Block methods rely on some stability and enough effective blocks. A long block cannot create additional crashes. The stationary bootstrap uses random block lengths. A fixed-length block simulation has a different sampling law. [Politis and Romano](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1994.10476870) |
| Do regime, recency and flow supply incremental information? | Start with one baseline and three fixed challengers. Fit parameters on resolved training observations only. Add one feature at a time and report paired out-of-sample score and cost-adjusted payoff differences. Consider beta-binomial shrinkage for sparse groups only after checking the Bernoulli and exchangeability assumptions. | Mutual information can reflect redundancy, discretization or leakage. Conditional mutual information needs enough distinct joint observations. Use a blockwise null and rerun selection inside training. A regularized logistic baseline precedes boosted trees, ensembles and hierarchical models. [Kraskov et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0305641) · [Vapnik and Chervonenkis](https://doi.org/10.1137/1116025) |
| When should a model be reviewed? | Log forward losses relative to baseline and execution-cost residuals. Develop a one-sided CUSUM with frozen reference, drift allowance and threshold. Evaluate time to detection and false alarms on distinct sessions. A sustained alarm triggers review. | Repeated ordinary significance tests inflate false discoveries. HMMs, Kalman/particle filters and volatility models need observable real-time inputs, identifiable states and evidence beyond simpler rules. Filtered probabilities use history through decision time. Smoothed states fitted on the whole sample leak later data. [Page](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/41.1-2.100) · [Hamilton](https://doi.org/10.2307/1912559) |

Plan power around a minimum economically meaningful **net** difference. Estimate session-level dispersion and clustering on development data, then simulate the registered test under that effect and under a zero-effect null. Report the number of usable sessions, effective blocks and chance of detecting the specified effect under those assumptions. Choose a sample horizon before opening the holdout. If that horizon cannot distinguish an economically relevant effect from noise, report insufficient evidence. Fitting an extreme-value tail, an optimal stopping policy, or a dynamic control law adds parameters at precisely the point where observations are scarce.

Check schema, trading date and capture pipeline before combining these with vendor-normalized data. Archive source checksums and correction dates. Databento documented duplicate-trade and OHLCV corrections in its 2026 history. Preserve which vintage produced each result. An individual can separate code paths, reserve a holdout, keep failed results, and request a human review. Deterministic roles using the same data can still share a defect. Stronger evidence requires an independent source reconciliation and a prospectively frozen forward run. A committee hierarchy or GPU cluster adds little to the current decision. The first useful experiment is a single preregistered setup on licensed, contract-specific observations with a documented execution model and a locked comparison period.
