# Frozen adaptive failed-break study

Use the same contract selection, complete sessions and Monday–Thursday MNQ archive as the opening-range study. Freeze the 09:30–09:59 ET high and low. From 10:00 to 14:28 inclusive, fade the first minute that trades outside just one end of that range and closes strictly inside. Buy a failed low, sell a failed high. Enter next open, one MNQ, stop 12 points, target 18 points, time exit at the open 30 minutes after entry. At most one proposal/day. Use existing conservative gap/both-hit handling. Deduct $3.50 round trip, stress $5.50.

Classify the last 20 close-to-close moves through signal time as trending in the direction of net movement when absolute net movement / total absolute movement >= 0.55; otherwise range. Use the four existing worker forecasts with equal fixed weights. Require 10 strictly prior resolved observations for the same contract, side and geometry. Learn from all resolved hypothetical proposals, including abstentions. Enter only if mean forecast exceeds (24 + 5.50) / (24 + 36). This screening threshold uses ideal barrier payoffs. Actual simulated P&L includes time exits, costs and gaps. Benchmark against cash, taking every proposal and the baseline worker alone using the same warmup and threshold.

One new policy, no tuning. First 598 sessions for development/warmup, next 200 for validation. Continue only if validation has at least 30 adaptive trades, positive stressed net P&L and strictly positive paired stressed net gains over both trading benchmarks. Otherwise reject and stop. If passed, repeat on final 200 reused sessions, explicitly exploratory. For promotion require a disjoint, previously unused sample after Oct 9 2025 with at least 100 eligible sessions and 30 adaptive trades, all these gates plus positive lower bounds on stressed expectancy and paired session gains against each trading benchmark. Circular blocks of three sessions, 19999 draws, seed 247, one-sided tail .05/(3 studied strategies × 3 endpoints). Failing any gate keeps the policy out of trading. No automatic retuning.
