## Account data contract

The example file lists the exact keys for the fixed-floor model. Each non-comment line is one case-sensitive `key=value`. Blank values, duplicate keys, unknown fields, fractional integers and missing prerequisites fail closed. Use a pseudonymous account ID and keep actual snapshots outside version control. All money is signed decimal integer USD cents. Price inputs are legal integer ticks. An unrepresentable exact calculation returns `DATA_UNVERIFIED`.

- **Identity and evidence.** Supply `provenance=human_attested` only after a human checks the specific account, contract, stage, and rules against contemporaneous evidence. Record retrievable evidence identifiers in `account_source`, `rule_source` and `execution_assumptions_source`. Use `illustrative` for fixtures. Identical `account_id` and `rule_account_id` bind the rule package to the ledger. The program checks internal consistency. Authentication and source verification require the operator. Stage must be `evaluation`, `funded` or `payout_eligible`.
- **Time and status.** All account, rule, exposure, cost and eligible-limit inputs must apply as of the same `account_observed_at_ms`. A snapshot may be up to 30 seconds old and a proposal up to 120 seconds old as of the supplied evaluation instant. Future inputs are rejected. `account_active` and `day_trading_enabled` must reflect prior terminal breaches, lockouts, resets, payouts and session transitions. Recompute on every fill, pending-order, equity, floor or rule change. The program has no platform connection.
- **Equity.** Reconcile nominal starting capital + signed non-P&L cash adjustments since inception + realized net P&L since inception + current unrealized P&L = current marked equity. Include fees exactly once in realized net P&L. Flat accounts must have zero unrealized P&L.
- **Floor.** For `fixed_marked_floor`, the official floor must equal the verified `fixed_floor_cents`. For `eod_trailing_marked_floor`, replace that single fixed-floor field with `highest_eod_reference_cents`, `trailing_distance_cents` and `floor_cap_cents` (an integer or the literal `none`). The reference is the historical high including initial capital and completed EOD marks through the last applicable close. The current floor is high minus distance, capped at the optional lock level. Match the result exactly to the official floor. Both models use continuous failure on a touched marked-equity floor and a daily lock on touching session-start marked equity minus the daily limit. A firm with intraday trailing peaks, realized-only limits, intraday cash resets or different accounting needs another verified rule model.
- **Additional exposure.** `incremental_open_pending_downside_cents` measures further loss from the current mark across all open positions and pending orders under the adverse scenario. The equity reconciliation already includes current unrealized loss. Record zero only for a flat account with no pending orders. A nonempty book needs quantified positive downside. `simultaneous_exposure_permitted` must reflect both account and personal rules.
- **Budgets.** `gross_daily_headroom_cents` is checked against the session-start equity and daily loss limit. `gross_personal_daily_remaining_cents` represents the remaining personal loss budget before reserving incremental open/pending downside. Capacity computation subtracts this downside once from each applicable gross limit. Floor/daily boundaries retain one cent where a touch would breach. A configured positive floor reserve can satisfy that one-cent requirement. The per-trade ceiling may be any amount from zero through $100. Two trades or two successive losses block further review. Count actual entry trades across all instruments in the applicable firm session.
- **Contract limits.** Supply the whole number of *additional* contracts permitted in the proposed product under firm aggregate exposure, personal size and available buying power, respectively. Precompute each residual allowance against all positions and pending orders, including firm micro/mini equivalence rules. Zero prohibits new exposure. All three must use the product in `limits_for_instrument`, verified margin schedule and actual contract month. The gate cannot derive netting or margin concessions from an undocumented rule.
- **Proposal and eligibility.** Include an explicit quarterly delivery month, verified last-tradable timestamp, long/short direction, entry and structural stop prices in ticks, and positive integer request. Establish the applicable trading session, predefined setup and the current independent evidence decision outside the sizing engine. `edge_review=insufficient` abstains. `independently_reviewed` requires a retrievable `edge_review_source`. In an illustrative fixture this only exercises code paths. These flags summarize external checks. They cannot establish forecast accuracy.

## Sizing and stress

Both instruments trade outright at 0.25 index points per tick. MNQ is $0.50 per tick and NQ is $5 per tick. The reported value must match the exchange specification. Off-grid prices require correction at the source. Integer tick arithmetic avoids rounding the stop loss down. [CME MNQ rule 36102](https://www.cmegroup.com/content/dam/cmegroup/rulebook/CME/IV/350/361.pdf) · [CME NQ rule 35902](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/CME/IV/350/359/359.pdf)

For one contract, planned variable loss is the tick distance × tick value, plus stressed adverse slippage × tick value, round-trip variable fees and other variable costs. Stressed variable loss further includes the independently specified failed-stop overshoot in ticks. Enter positive incremental overshoot and document the stress justification. If recent comparable observed adverse slippage exceeds the calibrated slippage input, the gate abstains for recalibration. The chosen stress remains a scenario. Larger gaps and failed executions can exceed it.

Subtract order-level fixed round-trip cost once from the remaining risk budget, then divide by per-contract stressed variable loss and round down. The maximum also respects residual firm, personal, margin, trade-count and simultaneous-exposure limits. An oversized proposal returns zero review contracts and reports the mathematical maximum for inspection. It never changes a stop or silently substitutes another size.

The JSON separates input provenance, the account, proposal geometry and numerical sizing from the final status and `review_contracts`. A nonzero `mathematical_max_under_limits` is a conditional arithmetic result. Only `RESEARCH_REVIEW_ONLY` can populate a nonzero `review_contracts`. Every status is advisory. Rejections identify the first blocking reason. The legacy `Risk.check`/`Quant.review` interface remains an MNQ-only historical heuristic with older input types. Use the survival gate for the account decision report.
